# Kobags site outline

## Current public launch scope — 20 October 2026

The bag builder is local-only pending the real bag scans. Public hero actions lead to manufacturing and **Choose your bag**. The interactive showroom and studio links are hidden; product pages lead directly to quotation requests. `/design` and `/sq/design` temporarily redirect to their catalogue equivalents. The catalogue, stock collections, manufacturing, responsibility, contact, partner and quote journeys remain public. The earlier outline below records the complete planned experience, including the locally retained studio.


## Brand homepage — `/`

1. Original production-facility photograph and “Manufacturing bags. Building brands.” headline.
2. Two clear hero paths: explore production and design your bag; direct jump to the 3D showroom.
3. Manufacturing origin, custom flexographic printing and wholesale guidance.
4. Factory/process introduction with original printing video, under user-controlled playback.
5. Interactive 3D showroom: rotate the tote, open its construction, choose a color/name and continue into the detailed designer.
6. The five original bag families and all 15 published sizes.
7. Choose → quote → artwork approval → production / dispatch.
8. Industry use cases, materials, certificate evidence, FAQ, resources and partners.
9. Complete company/footer navigation.

## Buyer destinations

- `/products` — five-family catalog, filtering and comparison.
- `/products/[slug]` — each product's specifications and all three published sizes (15 variants total).
- `/design` — dedicated interactive design landing page, logo upload, front/back preview and download.
- `/quote` — four-step quotation request preserving the design.
- `/samples` — separate sample inquiry.
- `/industries`, `/industries/retail` — application-led selection.
- `/manufacturing` — real factory imagery and printing video.
- `/sustainability`, `/certifications` — material facts and scoped evidence.
- `/partners`, `/partners/apply` — the three original partner models and application.
- `/resources` — buying / artwork guidance and downloads.
- `/blog` and six `/blog/[slug]` pages — practical journal with original article slugs retained.
- `/contact` — general inquiry and public company contacts.
- `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/cookies` — explicit demo policies and draft controls.

## Demonstration and handoff

- `/demo/customer` — requests, quotation acceptance, artwork approval and repeat requests.
- `/demo/admin` — sales, content, secondary inquiries and notification previews.
- `/handoff` — clickable map, review sequence and downloadable handoff documents.

The dedicated design studio is the new capability layered into the original business structure. The homepage remains the main brand landing page. Certification validity, environmental claims and customer-logo permissions require client evidence before production publication.

## Guest demonstration

Design → save request → prepare fictional quote and artwork → accept quote → approve artwork → place simulated order → optional reorder. No login required. Customer and sales views share only the current visitor’s demo records.
