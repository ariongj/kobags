# Studio image provenance

Original Kobags product photos retain their existing brand print and appear in the catalog.

The designer uses a separately generated 1983 × 793 atlas of five blank, neutral non-woven bag illustrations. It was generated with the imagegen tool as a standalone illustrative mockup after local reference-file editing failed in the environment. The designer selects each bag region at runtime, applies color, and overlays the buyer's artwork.

The atlas is not presented as a product photograph or exact manufactured geometry. The interface and exported preview label it as illustrative and require approval of actual shape, color, size and print position. Factory images, original product photographs, certificate scans and manufacturing video are genuine assets from Kobags' public site; see asset-sources.json.
