# Kobags website audit — 13 October 2026

## Fixes

- **Stock confirmations survive refresh.** Previously, a prepared request existed only in the current screen state. Its receipt now reloads from the current browser's saved request, with its original specifications and email handoff.
- **Interrupted requests can be retried.** Unchanged retries reuse the saved request; edited retries recover from an idempotency conflict without asking the buyer to submit again. Concurrent submission handlers are blocked.
- **Unavailable saved stock choices explain what to do.** The buyer is asked to select another available option, while their contact details remain in the form.
- **Form limits match the server.** Contact and country fields no longer accept values that are too long for the request API. Receipt-fetch errors and known request errors are surfaced in the selected language.
- **Receipt links respect browser ownership.** Another browser cannot open the original buyer's prepared receipt. It gets a fresh form with working draft persistence.
- **Handoff dates stay current.** The source manifest now records the packaging date in the Tirana timezone.
