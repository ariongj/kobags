KOBAGS GROUP — BUYER CHECKLIST
Private demonstration. Production guidance requires client approval.

1. Bag family and named size. Preserve the published dimension string.
2. Intended use, contents and weight requirements.
3. Non-woven PP material color and GSM.
4. Print colors (published product guidance: flexographic, up to four).
5. Front and back logo, placement concept and color reference.
6. Quantity. Published wholesale minimum: 5,000 bags; scope to confirm.
7. Destination country/city and requested arrival date.
8. Company and contact details.
9. Any custom dimensions, sample needs or special requirements.

Before production: confirm approved dimensional drawing, printable area,
artwork revision, commercial quotation, any payment condition, and timing.
A studio preview is not production-ready artwork.
