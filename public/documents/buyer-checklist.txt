KOBAGS GROUP — WHOLESALE BUYER CHECKLIST

Prepare these details for your quotation:

1. Bag family and size: D-Cut, Handle, Box, T-Shirt or String.
2. Intended contents, carrying needs and any load requirements.
3. Non-woven PP fabric weight (GSM) and preferred bag color.
4. Front and back artwork, placement and color references.
5. Number of print colors: published guidance is up to four.
6. Quantity per design, size and color. Wholesale starts at 5,000 bags;
   confirm how the minimum applies to your combination.
7. Destination city/country and the date you need the bags to arrive.
8. Company name and the person responsible for approving the order.
9. Sample needs, custom dimensions or other requirements.

Before production, agree the dimensional drawing, printable area, artwork
revision, price, payment terms and schedule. Screen colors are approximate;
a studio preview is a visual brief, not a production artwork file.

Dispatch and arrival are separate dates. Confirm transport and any
applicable customs arrangements in the quotation.
